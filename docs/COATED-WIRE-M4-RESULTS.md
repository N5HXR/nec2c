# M4 coating loss and frequency sweeps

Status: accepted 2026-11-05

Date: 2026-11-05

## Material evaluation

M4 promotes both loss fields in the frozen `LD 7` interface.  At every `FR`
frequency, the coating operator now evaluates

```text
epsilon_c / epsilon_0 =
    ER - i*(ER*TAN_DELTA + SIGMA_DC/(omega*epsilon_0)).
```

Consequently, `TAN_DELTA` remains constant over a sweep while the loss caused
by `SIGMA_DC` varies as `1/f`.  The evaluated complex relative permittivity is
recorded in each execution's `material_evaluations` array.  A tangent input and
its equivalent conductivity at a selected frequency produce the same operator
and results to the M4 target of `1e-12` relative.

The matrix order, current unknowns, and radiation operator remain unchanged.
The M3 coefficient `1-1/ER` becomes the complex, frequency-dependent
`1-1/epsilon_c`.  Zero loss therefore follows the accepted M3 path exactly.

## Absorbed power

The radial-field model gives the coating loss directly from the charge basis.
For each segment, the existing NEC sine and cosine current coefficients provide
the derivative of current and hence line charge.  M4 analytically integrates
the squared charge basis along the segment and the radial field energy between
the conductor radius `a` and coating radius `b`.  For line charge `lambda`,
the absorbed power per unit length is

```text
omega*epsilon_loss*|lambda|^2*log(b/a)
------------------------------------------------.
        4*pi*epsilon_0*|epsilon_c|^2
```

This value is reported as `power.dielectric_absorbed_w`, independently of the
far-zone radiation integral.  It is not folded into structure or network loss.
The air-radiated power is integrated over a 3,896-direction equal-area sphere;
their residual against accepted power is the reported balance defect.

For the 475 mm article at 276.65 MHz, `ER=3.2`, and `tan(delta)=0.05`, M4
currently reports:

| Quantity | Result |
| --- | ---: |
| Feed impedance | 65.807991 - j0.164894 ohm |
| Accepted power | 0.007597814 W |
| Dielectric absorption | 0.000154255 W |
| Air-radiated power | 0.007449807 W |
| Relative balance defect | 0.08224% |

Across the 250--320 MHz low-loss and lossy acceptance sweeps, the largest
balance defect is 0.13540%, below the milestone limit of 0.3%.  Absorbed power
is nonnegative at every tested frequency.

## OpenEMS comparison

OpenEMS represents dielectric loss with fixed conductivity.  Its nominal
`tan(delta)=0.05` material at the 285 MHz excitation centre therefore has
`kappa=0.002536842126 S/m`; the NEC comparison uses that value as `SIGMA_DC`
rather than incorrectly comparing it with a constant-tangent sweep.

The M1-qualified cylindrical geometry was retained: 2 mm split-ring source,
2 mm conductor radius, 4 mm flush coating radius, eight radial coating cells,
and `ER=3.2`.  A 185--385 MHz excitation centred at 285 MHz shortened the
transient while densely sampling the 250--320 MHz acceptance band.  The run
crossed -49.46 dB before settling to a small wideband late-time floor and was
postprocessed at -46.44 dB.  M1 independently established that the less strict
-40 dB result differs from its -50 dB result by only 0.0191% in resonance and
0.00047 ohm in resistance, so this run remains inside the qualified numerical
uncertainty.

| Quantity | NEC M4 | OpenEMS | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Resonance | 276.715410 MHz | 275.575091 MHz | +0.4138% |
| Resistance at resonance | 65.903734 ohm | 65.289400 ohm | +0.614333 ohm |
| Radiation efficiency | 97.9960% | 97.7648% | +0.2313 percentage point |

The lossy result therefore remains within M3's 1% resonance and 2 ohm
impedance-component envelope.  Its independently evaluated efficiency also
agrees within one percentage point.

## Tests and acceptance artifacts

`tests/coated-wire/m4_loss.py` checks tangent/conductivity equivalence,
per-frequency evaluation, passivity, zero-loss compatibility, dense-backend
equivalence, and the power-balance limit.  The user-facing sweeps are:

* `tests/coated-wire/m4-low-loss-sweep.nec`, `tan(delta)=0.002`;
* `tests/coated-wire/m4-lossy-sweep.nec`, `tan(delta)=0.05`; and
* `tests/coated-wire/m4-openems-equivalent-sweep.nec`, constant conductivity
  matching the OpenEMS material at its 285 MHz design frequency.

The compact record and plots are generated by
`tools/coated_wire_m4_acceptance.py`.  M4 is complete only after the OpenEMS
comparison is recorded and the user reviews the two VSWR sweeps and gives an
explicit PASS verdict.
